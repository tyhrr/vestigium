Gallery

Life at Vestigium in pictures: the Saturday market, workshops, swaps, evenings in the library and days in the Zelenica garden. Everything here is made by neighbours, for neighbours.

What happens at Vestigium

  • EKO PLAC

    Every Saturday 9–11 · Rudeška 142

    Small local and organic farmers sell directly to the neighbourhood: bread, cheese, honey, seasonal vegetables and flowers.

  • Workshops

    Sewing, weaving, cooking, art

    Neighbours teach neighbours: from the little sewing school and textile upcycling to healthy cooking and painting.

  • Community life

    Free library · swaps · game nights

    A living room for the neighbourhood: book exchange, monthly clothes swaps, board games, concerts and talks.

  • Zelenica & outdoors

    Brezovica · gardens · walks

    The cooperative garden in Brezovica, evenings by the fire, flower beds, murals and walks in nature.
