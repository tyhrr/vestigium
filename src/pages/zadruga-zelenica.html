Zadruga Zelenica

The Zelenica platform was launched in 2016 to guide society from unsustainable to sustainable practices that support people, nature and fair distribution. It connects organic and ecological food producers with citizens.

In 2021 Zelenica became a social cooperative, and in 2022 the Cooperative Community Center Brezovica opened. We continue gathering members and developing the audience.
