    About Vestigium

    Vestigium (lat. footprint) was born in 2011 by the initiative of six women who listened to the needs of their neighbors in the Vrbana area, Zagreb. We created a multifunctional space where initiatives can develop and flourish.

    As part of the Global Transition Movement for Sustainable Communities, we work to solve problems such as the growing distance between people, the lack of meeting and socializing places, and the exclusion of families and elderly residents.

    Since 2017 we are beneficiaries of institutional support from the National Foundation for Civil Society Development. In 2017 we were presented as an inspiring example "Changemakers" from Croatia, and in 2018 nominated among the top 30 projects for the "Social Marie Award" among 250 European socially innovative projects.

    About Vestigium

    Community Center

    Vestigium is a community center in the hands of citizens. We organize a free library, creative workshops, healthy cooking, recycling and environmental care, lectures and presentations of different cultures. We support short supply chains through EKO PLAC every Saturday 9-11h, swap clothes monthly, and evening yoga/fitness/dance classes. Spaces are available for coworking, celebrations and promotions.

    We operate in two locations: Community Center Vrbani, Rudeška 142, and Cooperative Community Center Zelenica, Grančarska 2a, Brezovica.

    Community Center Vrbani

    Rudeška 142, Zagreb. Free library, workshops, farmers market, swap shops and cultural events.

    Cooperative Center Zelenica

    Grančarska 2a, Brezovica. Cooperative hub for local food, culture and community initiatives.

    Zadruga Zelenica

    The Zelenica platform was launched in 2016 to guide society from unsustainable to sustainable practices that support people, nature and fair distribution. It connects organic and ecological food producers with citizens.

    In 2021 Zelenica became a social cooperative, and in 2022 the Cooperative Community Center Brezovica opened. We continue gathering members and developing the audience.
